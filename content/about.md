👋🏼 I'm a senior product designer in Vancouver with 9+ years of making complicated software easier to use. Right now I lead product design at MAVI, teach UX/UI at BCIT, and run a design community that got a little out of hand.

Chat with me about:

## 🏡 Community Builder

I started Vancouver Design Community because I wanted more designers to talk to. It's now 4,200+ people. We run socials, talks, workshops and portfolio reviews, and it's open to anyone who's curious about design, whatever your title says. [Come say hi in our Slack.](https://join.slack.com/t/vandesigncommunity/shared_invite/zt-4af1yypit-~LvOH0pQSN2XCb5oP_tG9A)

## 🍎 Teaching

I teach UX/UI design at BCIT, which is the same place I learned it. I love being in front of students, coaching them and watching them get sharper. We do hands-on workshops every week, and I run regular surveys and feedback so I know what they're thinking and feeling, not what I assume they are.

I also built Corporate Ascent for my classes, a challenge system where students take on design briefs the way a real team would.

## 📸 Moment Capturer

I held my first camera when I was 6 on a European trip with my parents and haven't stopped taking photos ever since. These days it's 5+ years of paid work: portraits, editorial, events, and portfolios for models and businesses. There's never a moment finer than one captured through a lens. [See the photos.](https://jarell.photography/)

## 🏐 Volleyball

I started playing in early 2025, on the beach, with no idea what I was doing. Now I play at a very high level and earned MVP with the first team I played on. Indoor is my favourite. It gets me sweating, moving and into a flow state, and it's taught me a lot about communication, teamwork and making smart decisions fast.
